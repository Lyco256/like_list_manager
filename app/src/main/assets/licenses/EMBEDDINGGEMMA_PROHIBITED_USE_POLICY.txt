Gemma Prohibited Use Policy

Canonical policy:
https://ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy

The bundled EmbeddingGemma model must not be used to:

- infringe, misappropriate, or otherwise violate another person's or entity's
  rights;
- facilitate dangerous, illegal, malicious, criminal, terrorist, or illegal
  substance-related activity;
- engage in illegal or unlicensed professional practice, including legal,
  medical, accounting, or financial practice;
- abuse, harm, interfere with, or disrupt services, including spam, scams,
  phishing, fraud, or malware;
- override or circumvent safety filters or intentionally contravene this
  policy;
- promote hatred, harassment, bullying, violence, self-harm, or harmful
  distribution of personally identifying information;
- track or monitor people without consent, or infer sensitive/private
  information without the rights, authorizations, and consents required by law;
- misinform, misrepresent provenance, impersonate people to deceive, make
  misleading claims of expertise, make automated decisions affecting rights
  or well-being, or generate defamatory content; or
- generate sexually explicit content for pornography or sexual gratification.

The complete policy, including its examples and any future updates, is
maintained at the canonical URL above and is incorporated by reference.
